# Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard

**Author:** Ajim Mujavar  
**Project Type:** Business Intelligence & Data Visualization  
**Tool:** Tableau Desktop

## Project Overview

The **Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard** is a business intelligence project developed using Tableau Desktop to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional trends, product categories, and customer segments.

The dashboard transforms business data into interactive visualizations and KPI summaries, helping users explore performance patterns and understand key business metrics through a clear, executive-friendly interface.

This project demonstrates practical skills in dashboard development, data visualization, KPI reporting, business analysis, and data storytelling.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze sales and profitability across regions.
- Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- Explore customer segment performance.
- Visualize geographic sales distribution where geographic data is available.
- Present business information through a clean, interactive dashboard.
- Support data-driven analysis and business decision-making.

## Key Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive KPI Summary
Displays important performance indicators in a consolidated layout. Examples include total sales, total profit, order quantity, and order count, depending on the final dashboard configuration.

### 2. Regional Performance Analysis
Helps compare sales and profitability across available geographic regions to identify differences in business performance.

### 3. Product Category Analysis
Provides a visual comparison of product categories and sub-categories to explore their contribution to sales and profit.

### 4. Geographic Visualization
Uses available geographic fields to display sales distribution and explore regional patterns.

### 5. Customer Segment Analysis
Allows users to compare business performance across available customer segments.

### 6. Interactive Dashboard Experience
Where configured, dashboard filters and interactive selections allow users to explore specific regions, categories, segments, and reporting periods.

## Business Questions

The dashboard can help investigate questions such as:

1. What are the total sales and profit for the selected reporting period?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales?
3. Which product categories contribute most to overall performance?
4. How does profitability vary across regions and product categories?
5. Which customer segments contribute significantly to sales?
6. What geographic patterns deserve further investigation?

Actual findings should be documented only after validating the results in Tableau.

## Dashboard Design Principles

The dashboard is designed with the following principles:

- Clear hierarchy between the dashboard title, KPIs, and charts.
- Consistent typography and chart headings.
- Restrained borders and a coordinated color palette.
- Appropriate spacing between dashboard components.
- Readable labels, tables, and legends.
- Fit settings selected to preserve chart readability.

## Data Quality & Validation

Before interpreting the dashboard, validate the source data, aggregation levels, calculated fields, number formats, and filters.

Particular attention should be given to:

- Missing or duplicate records.
- Sales and profit calculation definitions.
- Currency and number formatting.
- Distinct order counts versus row counts.
- Geographic field accuracy.
- Consistency of KPI totals with the underlying data.
